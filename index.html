yield-placebo

Debt and its psychology.

The security of extra returns is a mechanism designed to generate the illusion of active participation in the markets, on a highly paternalistic safety net.

1. Debt is not mathematics, it's psychology

Nobody buys debt. People buy peace of mind disguised as a smart decision. The average investor doesn't want returns; they want to avoid feeling foolish for doing nothing.

2. The Placebo Mechanism

The system allows you to earn an extra 1-2% on top of the base rate. It's not enough to make you rich, but it's enough to make you feel like you've beaten the system. That's the placebo. It makes you feel active when, in reality, you're sitting on a safety net.

3. The Paternalistic Network

Behind every "safe + extra" product is a daddy looking out for you: the central bank, the government, the bank that sold it to you, the implicit bailout guarantee. If everything goes to hell, daddy pays. You never see the real risk.

Conclusion

You're not paid for your skill. You're paid to stay. The extra yield is the cost of keeping you in the game without letting you really play.
