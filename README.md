# yield-placebo

> Debt and its psychology. The security of extra returns is a mechanism designed to generate the illusion of active participation in the markets, on a highly paternalistic safety net.

### Thesis in one line: You're not paid more for your skill. You're paid a little more so you think you're playing.

### The mechanism

**1. Psychological need:** Investors don't just want security; they want to feel like they're participating.

**2. Product design:** A "safe + extra" product is offered (corporate bond, Cete plus, high yield, staking).

**3. Illusion:** That +0.5% / +2% generates dopamine from the feeling of "I'm beating the market."

**4. Reality:** The tail risk continues to be absorbed by a paternalistic structure: central banking, implicit bailout, issuer guarantee.

 ### Formula

`Perceived Return = Base Rate + Alpha Illusion`
`Real Risk = Socialized by the Paternalistic Network`

### Why It Works

Because it's easier to sell security with emotion than emotion with security.
